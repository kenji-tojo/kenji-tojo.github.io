    \section{About}
        \begin{onecolentry}
            Kenji Tojo is a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Z\"urich, working with Bernd Bickel.
            He received his Ph.D. from the University of Tokyo, advised by Nobuyuki Umetani, where he specialized in computer graphics and computational design.
            He has published multiple first-author papers at top venues, including SIGGRAPH, and his research has been recognized with several awards, such as the Dean's Prize for Outstanding Master's and Doctoral Theses and a Best Paper Honorable Mention at SGP.
            His work bridges computer graphics, computer vision, and machine learning, with a particular focus on extending inverse rendering and differentiable graphics techniques to support a broader range of applications, including content creation and design optimization.
        \end{onecolentry}
